Company Overview

Who We Are

AMARIC ELITE Professional Cleaning & Pest Control Solutions is a Kenyan service company dedicated to delivering high-quality cleaning, sanitation, pest management, and environmental hygiene solutions to residential, commercial, institutional, and industrial clients.

Founded on the principles of professionalism, reliability, and service excellence, AMARIC ELITE was established to address the growing need for dependable cleaning and pest control services that prioritize client satisfaction, safety, and long-term value.

We believe that clean, healthy, and well-maintained environments contribute significantly to productivity, well-being, and peace of mind. Through skilled personnel, modern techniques, and customer-focused service delivery, we strive to create spaces where individuals, families, and organizations can thrive.

As a growing enterprise, AMARIC ELITE is committed to building long-lasting relationships with clients by consistently delivering effective solutions, maintaining high professional standards, and continuously improving our services to meet evolving customer needs.
